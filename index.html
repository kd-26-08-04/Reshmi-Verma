Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Clinical Inquiries

Learn how our integrative consultations work and what you can expect.

What is the Body Oxygen Level Test (BOLT) and why does it matter?
+
The BOLT test measures your carbon dioxide tolerance and physiological breathing efficiency. Carbon dioxide is not merely a waste gas—it triggers hemoglobin to release oxygen into your organs and brain cells via the Bohr Effect. A low BOLT score (< 20s) correlates strongly with airway collapse, anxiety, and fatigue.
How is functional nutrition different from standard dietitians?
+
We move far beyond obsolete calorie counting. Reshmi Verma analyzes nutrition as biochemical information—fueling mitochondrial ATP synthesis, repairing the gut mucosal barrier, and balancing glycemic kinetics based on verified functional diagnostics.
What happens in an Initial Discovery Call vs a Comprehensive Consultation?
+
The Initial Discovery Call (20 min) is a focused discussion to review your main health challenges, clarify questions, and determine the right clinical pathway. The Comprehensive Consultation (60 min) includes a deep dive into blood chemistry, gut history, circadian sleep mapping, and a full personalized protocol.
Can breathwork help with gut issues and acid reflux?
+
Yes, significantly. The vagus nerve controls both parasympathetic breathing and digestive motility/gastric acid release. Slow diaphragmatic breathing stimulates vagal tone, physically lowering the diaphragm, stabilizing the lower esophageal sphincter, and encouraging digestive enzyme secretion.
